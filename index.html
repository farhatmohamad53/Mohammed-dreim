My story

I never stopped.

When the path disappeared, I kept looking for a way forward.

“I don’t want the circumstances I was born into to determine how far I can go.”

I’m Mohamed Farhat, a swimmer from Gaza, and swimming has always been more than a sport to me. It is the place where I feel free, where hard work has meaning, and where I can imagine a future beyond the circumstances around me.

For years, I have carried a dream of reaching the highest levels of competitive swimming. But living through war and difficult circumstances has made that dream incredibly hard to pursue. I lost access to regular training and to the sports facilities and resources that an athlete needs to improve and compete at a high level.

Still, I never stopped. Whenever I can get into the water, I train. I keep going with whatever resources are available to me, because I refuse to let the circumstances I was born into decide how far I can go.

Today, I am asking for an opportunity — not for someone else to achieve my dream for me, but for the chance to fight for it myself. Support can help me return to better training, obtain essential swimming equipment, improve my performance, and keep moving toward the future I have worked so hard for.

Every contribution matters. Every share matters. Even a small act of kindness can become part of a much bigger opportunity.

My first goal is $10,000. I hope that, together, we can turn determination into opportunity and hope into achievement. Thank you for believing that a young swimmer from Gaza deserves a chance to dream — and a chance to chase that dream.

“I’m not asking you to achieve my dream for me. I’m asking for the opportunity to fight for it myself.”
